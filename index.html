Quick setup

From download to playing in minutes.

The release is self-contained. Keep the executable and bundled-mods folder together after extracting.

↓ Download JOJO Client
  1. 01

    Download

    Save the current Windows x64 ZIP from this page.

  2. 02

    Extract everything

    Right-click the ZIP, choose Extract All, and keep the bundled-mods folder beside the app.

  3. 03

    Open JOJO Client

    Run JOJO Client.exe and choose how much memory Minecraft can use. This personal build is not code-signed, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning.

  4. 04

    Sign in and play

    Use your Microsoft account locally, then press Play JOJO Client.
